NH Repairs

Commercial Truck, Trailer & RV Repair

Acquired May 2025
Structure Carve-out Acquisition
Role Owner-Operator
Location Pompano Beach, FL

Transaction Overview

Angular Partners acquired the repair and maintenance division from Nationwide Haul, a diversified equipment dealer operating across the United States. The carve-out involved separating the non-core repair operations from the parent company's core equipment sales, leasing, and financing business.

The Business

NH Repairs specializes in commercial truck, trailer, and RV repair services, serving fleet operators, owner-operators, and individual customers throughout South Florida. The facility operates multiple service bays with a team of experienced diesel technicians.

Post-Acquisition Strategy

Following the acquisition, the business has been revitalized through:

  • Hands-on Leadership: Daily involvement in operations, customer relationships, and strategic decision-making
  • Strategic Repositioning: Sharpened focus on high-margin commercial fleet services and emergency repair work
  • Disciplined Operations: Implemented process improvements, parts inventory management, and technician scheduling optimization
  • Commercial Strategy: Redesigned pricing structure, customer acquisition approach, and vendor relationships

Growth Vision

The focus is on building a scalable platform for fleet maintenance and repair services in South Florida, with plans to pursue strategic add-on acquisitions in the diesel repair sector. The goal is to consolidate fragmented independent shops and create operational efficiencies through shared infrastructure, bulk purchasing, and centralized management.

Mudshare

Political Communications Platform

Acquired 2023
Structure Lionfield Capital + Syndicate
Role CEO
Exited 2024

Transaction Overview

Acquired through Lionfield Capital and a syndicate of investors, Mudshare was a leading Civictech company powering enhanced deliverability and compliance of marketing communications in the political space.

The Business

Founded in 2014 by David Cerrone, Mudshare became a trailblazing communications software platform facilitating engagement, fundraising, and awareness for political campaigns and advocacy organizations through its unique Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) model. The platform specialized in SMS and geofencing technology, enabling highly targeted political communications.

Operating Role

As CEO, led the company through a critical growth phase, focusing on:

  • Product Expansion: Enhanced the platform's geofencing capabilities and compliance features
  • Market Positioning: Repositioned Mudshare for the evolving political technology landscape
  • Team Retention: Maintained all key employees and collaborators post-acquisition to preserve institutional knowledge
  • Strategic Exit: Successfully executed an exit to strategic buyers

Key Takeaways

The Mudshare acquisition demonstrated the ability to source, structure, close, and operate a complex software business in a highly specialized vertical. The transaction required navigating regulatory compliance issues, understanding political campaign cycles, and managing a distributed technology team—all while driving growth and positioning the company for a successful exit.
