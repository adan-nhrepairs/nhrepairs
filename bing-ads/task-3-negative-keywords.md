# TASK 3 — NEGATIVE KEYWORD MASTER LIST
## NH Repairs | RV Repair — Phone Call Volume

---

## HOW TO APPLY IN MICROSOFT ADS

1. Go to **Tools → Negative Keyword Lists**
2. Click **Create List**
3. Name: `NH Repairs — RV Campaign Exclusions`
4. Paste all keywords below (one per line)
5. Save the list
6. Navigate to **Campaigns → [RV Repair Campaign] → Keywords → Negative Keywords**
7. Click **Add from list** and apply the list
8. Also apply at the **Account level** for blanket protection

**Match type rule:** Use **broad match negatives** unless specified — they block more variations.
For brand names and specific terms you want hard-blocked regardless of word order, use exact match negatives `[keyword]`.

## ONGOING NEGATIVE KEYWORD MANAGEMENT

**Weekly task (first 90 days):**
1. Go to **Reports → Search Term Report** in Microsoft Ads
2. Download all search terms that triggered your ads
3. Identify irrelevant queries
4. Add them as negatives immediately

**What to look for:**
- Towable RV terms that slipped through
- Parts-only searches
- DIY/how-to queries
- Out-of-area terms
- Job searches
- Any non-repair intent

**Target:** Search term irrelevancy rate below 10% by day 60.
