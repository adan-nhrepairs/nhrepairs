Frequently Asked Questions

Will my information be kept confidential?

Absolutely. All conversations and information shared are kept strictly confidential. We understand the sensitivity of business transitions and respect your privacy completely.

What types of businesses are you looking to acquire?

We focus on profitable, cash-flowing businesses in blue-collar service industries, particularly diesel repair and fleet maintenance. Ideal businesses have $500K-$5M in revenue, consistent cash flow, and are located in South Florida or the Southeast.

How long does the acquisition process typically take?

Most transactions close within 60-90 days from signing a Letter of Intent. However, we're flexible and can work with your timeline to ensure a smooth transition.

Do you work with business brokers?

Yes, we work with professional business brokers and respect their role in the process. We're happy to connect directly with brokers who have relevant opportunities.

What if I'm not ready to sell but want to explore options?

That's perfectly fine. We're happy to have exploratory conversations with no pressure or commitment. Many business owners benefit from understanding their options well before making a decision.
